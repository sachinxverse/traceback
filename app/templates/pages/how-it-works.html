How TRACEBACK works

Matching you can trust, decisions made by people

Our engine is fast, but the final word is always human. Here is the full journey of a lost item, from report to reunion.

Matching

When you submit a report, we compare it with reports of the opposite type using four signals: image similarity (OpenCLIP), wording similarity (Sentence Transformers), location proximity and time proximity. Every match page shows the score breakdown of each signal — nothing is hidden in a black box.

Claims

A match is only a recommendation. To recover an item, the owner submits a claim with details only the true owner would know — serial numbers, scratches, exact contents. The finder — or the institution holding the item — compares those details side by side and decides.

Privacy

Contact details are never shared between users. Case messaging opens only after a claim exists, so conversations always have a real reason. Private identifying details are visible only to the reviewer and platform admins — never to match counterparts.

Human verification

No automated decision ever approves a claim or establishes ownership. Handover is confirmed by the reviewer, receipt is confirmed by the claimant, and both reports are marked recovered only then.
