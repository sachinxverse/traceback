How it works

Matching

When you submit a report, we compare it with reports of the opposite type using image similarity, wording similarity, location proximity and time proximity. A ranked list of candidate matches appears on your report page with a score breakdown.

Claims

A match is only a recommendation. To recover an item the owner submits a claim with details only the true owner would know (serial numbers, scratches, contents). The finder — or the institution holding the item — reviews and approves or rejects.

Privacy

Contact details are never shared between users. Case messaging opens only after a claim exists. Private details are visible only to the reviewer and admins.

Human verification

No automated decision ever approves a claim or establishes ownership. Every handover is confirmed by a person.
