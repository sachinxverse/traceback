# Privacy Policy — TRACEBACK Last updated: 2026-11-17. This policy describes what the app collects, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to delete it. ## What we collect - **Account:** name, email address, optional phone number, hashed password (never the password itself). - **Reports:** item title, description, category, date/time, GPS coordinates or place text, and up to a few photos you upload. Photos are stripped of EXIF metadata (including location) before storage. - **Optional private details** on a report (serial numbers, marks): visible only to the report owner and the claim reviewer. - **Operational records:** a security audit log of sensitive actions, and one-time-code login records (codes are stored hashed, never readable). ## Who sees what - Your name is shown publicly as **first name only**. - Your email/phone are **never** shown to other users; in-app chat is the only contact channel, and messages are visible to the two participants (and admins for abuse handling). - Report photos are served only to signed-in users allowed to see the report; they are never public URLs. - Staff of an institution see reports tied to their institution; admins see everything for moderation and the audit log. ## Retention - Reports auto-expire **180 days** after the incident date (configurable `REPORT_RETENTION_DAYS`) and are purged by a scheduled cleanup task. - Chat messages are purged 30 days after a claim closes. - Login one-time codes expire within minutes and are purged daily. - Backups (managed Postgres / GitHub Actions artifacts) rotate within 14 days. ## Deleting your data - **Self-service:** Profile → "Delete my account". This immediately revokes all your sessions, anonymises your account (name replaced with "Deleted User", email/phone replaced with non-identifying placeholders) and keeps the community's lost/found records and the security audit trail, which are required for dispute handling. - **Full erasure request:** contact the operators via the address on record in the repository owner's GitHub profile; requests are processed within 30 days except where retention is legally required (audit records).
